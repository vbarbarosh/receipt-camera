# Phone

## One-time setup

1. Phone and laptop on the same wifi.
2. Open the printed URL (e.g. `http://192.168.1.23:8080/`) in Chrome.
3. Menu ⋮ → **Add to Home screen** — you get a "Receipts" icon that opens the app fullscreen.

## Daily use

Open the icon, tap **Take picture** — the phone's camera app opens at full
quality. Take the shot, confirm it, and the photo uploads automatically: you
hear a shutter click and see "saved receipt_…" when it is on the laptop.

After each confirmed shot the camera reopens for the next receipt — either
automatically, or (if Chrome blocks that) via a full-screen "tap anywhere for
next receipt" page. To stop the loop, cancel/press Back in the camera app, or
tap **done** on the tap-anywhere page.
